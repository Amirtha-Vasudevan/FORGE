    Grading final answers no longer proves learning.

    AI produces correct answers instantly. Writing tools already capture the writing process, but nothing equivalent exists for code, derivations or lab work, where faking a final answer is even easier.

    Easy to fake

    The final answer

    Submitted at the end. Looks the same whether a student or an AI produced it, and shows nothing about reasoning.

    Hard to fake

    The process

    Every edit, derivation step and observation, captured over time as evidence of real effort.

    Three modules, one evidence layer.

    Code

    Replays edit history, detects pasted blocks and burst typing, tracks test-run progress and compares versions with AST-diff.

    Math

    Checks each derivation step symbolically with SymPy and tests whether the reasoning stays consistent from line to line.

    Lab

    Verifies timestamped observation logs, data-entry consistency and the integrity of recorded results.

    Instructors can see how a student reached a solution, check understanding with follow-up questions, grade with evidence, and spot weak areas across a whole batch. FORGE is a learning-evidence tool, not a surveillance tool.

    From keystroke to explainable score.

    Capture

    Students work in a secure workspace. Edits, steps and logs stream live.

    Analyze

    Code, math and lab engines look for paste bursts, broken reasoning and inconsistent data.

    Verify

    Instructors get a Process Authenticity Score with traceable evidence, never a black-box verdict.
